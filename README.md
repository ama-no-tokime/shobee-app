# Shobee (Android test build)

Shobee is an AI-assisted mobile shop for electronics, built as a Final Year Project at Tunku Abdul Rahman University of Management and Technology (TAR UMT). Buyers can search by describing what they need, ask an AI assistant for advice, and pay sellers by Touch 'n Go transfer.

This repository only holds the Android app for **user testing**. The source code is kept private.

## Download

Go to [**Releases**](../../releases/latest) and download `Shobee-v0.1.0.apk`.

## Install on Android

1. Open the downloaded `Shobee-v0.1.0.apk` on your Android phone (Android 10 or newer).
2. If your phone blocks it, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager when asked.
3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, choose **Install anyway**. The app is a student project and is not published on the Play Store.
4. Open **Shobee** and create a buyer account. A 6-digit code is sent to your email to verify it.

## Help with testing

1. Try these tasks in the app:
   1. Register a buyer account and verify it with the email code.
   2. Search in plain words (for example "laptop for university") and open a product.
   3. Ask the AI assistant a question (for example "phone with long battery life").
   4. Add an RM 1 product to the cart and open the Touch 'n Go payment page.
   5. Open **My orders** and check the order status.
2. Then fill in the questionnaire (about 10 minutes):
   **https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd34Xs8SBLQZvzFIZR58BgB09U8fcf1TwApt_F7j16xto8k9Q/viewform**

## Important

- **Payments are real.** The Touch 'n Go payment page shows a real e-wallet QR code. You do **not** need to pay to finish the test: stop at the payment page, then cancel the order in **My orders**.
- Use a password you do not use anywhere else. Test accounts may be deleted after the project ends.
- The app needs an internet connection. The camera, location and fingerprint permissions are only used by seller and delivery-partner accounts.

Thank you for helping!
